    1. In Experiment, choose a starting scenario.

    Read the live display

    Membrane voltage
    The model neuron's voltage at the displayed time. Crossing threshold creates a recorded spike and reset.
    Open fraction
    The fraction of modeled receptors in a conducting state. It is different from ligand binding.
    GABA-A current
    Signed drive on the membrane: positive is depolarizing and negative is hyperpolarizing at that moment. Conductance can also shunt excitation.
    Peak this run
    The maximum recorded open fraction in the whole completed experiment, rather than the current playback value.
    Playback time
    Position in an already calculated experiment. Playback speed changes the animation pace, not the model's timescale.
    Understand this modelAssumptions, units & interpretation

    A continuous chain of calculations

    Transmitter release drives two separate diffusion and clearance models. Local concentration controls a simplified receptor open-state fraction, which sets synaptic conductance and changes membrane voltage.

    What a spike means here

    The leaky integrate-and-fire model records a threshold crossing, resets the voltage, and enforces a refractory period. It does not calculate the sodium/potassium action-potential waveform or axonal propagation.

    Interpret the controls

    Release dose is integrated concentration in µM·µm in an effective one-dimensional extracellular domain. Receptor availability is relative, not a receptor count. These illustrative settings are not a calibrated biological specimen.

    Inhibition depends on voltage

    GABA-A conductance drives voltage toward its selected reversal potential. This may hyperpolarize the neuron or reduce its response to excitation through shunting.
